# Live Play Layout

The Living Table is a play surface first and a management application second.

## Primary rule

The **Current Card Board is the first full-width surface** on both Dungeon Master and player routes.

During active play, the board answers the immediate questions:

- Where is the party?
- What Area are they in?
- Who is present?
- What threats, hazards, and rewards are currently relevant?
- What information has the Dungeon Master revealed?

## Visual order

### Dungeon Master route

1. Current Card Board
2. Compact brand and dice bar
3. Adventure Deck and Combat Initiative
4. Quest Tracker
5. Local-session save and recovery controls
6. Adventure / Scene navigation
7. Library tabs and preparation tools

### Player route

1. Revealed Card Board
2. Compact brand and dice bar
3. Character station and player-safe initiative
4. Quest Tracker
5. Library and supporting tools

## Product reasoning

The board is where both roles follow the game. Preparation, navigation, saving, browsing, and administration support the board; they must not push it below the fold or compete with it for first attention.

This hierarchy applies to desktop, tablet, and phone layouts. Smaller screens stack the same sequence vertically rather than changing the priority.
